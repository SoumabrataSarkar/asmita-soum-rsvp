Privacy

The wedding of Asmita Das & Soumabrata Sarkar · 26–28 January 2027

This is a private wedding invitation, made for friends and family. It is not a business, it sells nothing, and it exists only so that people we have invited can tell us whether they are coming.

What signing in with Google gives us

If you sign in with Google before answering, we receive two things and nothing else:

  • your name, as it appears on your Google account
  • your email address

We ask for this for one reason: so that the email address on an RSVP is genuinely yours, and we are not left guessing who a reply came from. We cannot see your password, your mail, your files, your contacts, your photographs or anything else in your account, and we never ask for access to them.

Signing in is optional. If it does not work on your phone, or you would rather not, there is a link under the button that opens the form anyway. Your answer reaches us either way.

What the form asks you

  • Your name, contact number and email address
  • Whether you are able to join us
  • The date you expect to arrive, and which celebrations you will come to
  • Whether you would like us to arrange accommodation
  • Any message you would like to leave us, and any songs you would like played

Where it goes, and who sees it

Answers are written into a private Google Sheet owned by us. Only the two of us and the family members helping us plan can open it. It is used for exactly one purpose: arranging this wedding — counting guests, booking rooms, ordering food, and playing your song requests.

We do not sell it, share it, publish it, or give it to anyone else. There is no advertising on this site, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The page sets no cookies of its own.

How long we keep it

Until the wedding is over and the arrangements are settled. After that the sheet is deleted.

If you want your answer removed

Ask us, and we will delete your row. No explanation needed. You can reach us at soumabrata.skr@gmail.com, or on the phone numbers at the bottom of the invitation.
